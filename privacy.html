Privacy

CS-E4740 Federated Learning · Aalto University

This site is a static course page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no advertising, and does not track visitors.

This website

The site is served by GitHub Pages as static files. GitHub records server log data, including IP addresses, for the operation and security of the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. No data is collected by the course maintainer through this site.

Follow-along registration

The free follow-along registration is operated with pretix at register.alexjung.eu. It collects the name and email address you enter, and your answers to the optional questions asked on the form. These are used only to send course announcements, assignment releases and live session reminders for this course edition, and to count registrations by country and referral channel for planning. They are not sold, and are not shared with third parties beyond the hosting provider.

For this list the controller is Alexander Jung (contact below), not Aalto University; enrolled students are covered by the university's notices, see the next section. The legal basis is your consent, given by submitting the form, and every message carries an unsubscribe link. The list is kept for the spring 2027 edition and deleted at the end of 2027 at the latest. You can ask at the contact address below, at any time, to see, correct, export or delete your entry, and you can lodge a complaint with the Finnish supervisory authority, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Enrolled Aalto students

Students enrolled through Sisu are covered by Aalto University's own privacy notices for teaching and study records, not by this page. Course assignments and project work are handled in Aalto's MyCourses and JupyterHub systems under those notices.

Lecture recordings

Recordings and live sessions are published on YouTube, which applies the Google Privacy Policy to viewers. Live chat messages are public while a session is live and in its archive. Participants never appear on camera or by name unless they choose to.

Google OAuth client

An OAuth client registered for this course is used by the maintainer alone, from a local script, to upload videos and set descriptions and thumbnails on the course YouTube channel. It is not offered to visitors, has no other users, and collects no data from anyone.
